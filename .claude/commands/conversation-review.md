Review my Claude conversation history from recent weeks to identify improvement opportunities and workflow optimizations.

**Analysis Framework:**

1. **Conversation Categorization** - Analyze patterns by interaction type:
   - Project management & planning sessions
   - Technical debugging & problem-solving
   - Code development & refactoring
   - Build system & infrastructure work
   - Architecture & design discussions

2. **Workflow Pattern Analysis** - Identify recurring workflows:
   - Look for repeated sequences of commands or approaches
   - Note successful problem-solving strategies
   - Identify time-consuming or repetitive processes
   - Find areas where automation could help

3. **Custom Command Opportunities** - Extract reusable workflows:
   - Multi-step processes that could be templated
   - Project-specific workflows worth standardizing
   - Cross-project patterns that could be generalized
   - Debugging sequences that follow consistent patterns

4. **CLAUDE.md Enhancement Opportunities** - Identify knowledge to codify:
   - Recurring technical issues and their solutions
   - Project-specific patterns and anti-patterns
   - Build/test procedures that get repeated
   - Architecture decisions and rationale

5. **Efficiency Improvements** - Find optimization opportunities:
   - Context switching patterns that could be streamlined
   - Information that gets re-discovered frequently
   - Planning vs. execution balance adjustments
   - Progress tracking and handoff improvements

**Deliverables:**

1. **Categorized conversation summary** with frequency analysis
2. **Recommended custom commands** (both project-specific and universal)
3. **CLAUDE.md additions** for global and project-level improvements
4. **Workflow optimization suggestions** based on observed patterns
5. **Implementation plan** for the most impactful improvements

**Focus Areas:**
- Look for patterns spanning multiple weeks/projects
- Prioritize improvements with highest frequency/impact
- Balance project-specific vs. universal improvements
- Consider both immediate wins and long-term workflow evolution
